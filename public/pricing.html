Questions

Before you choose

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move up or down at any time, and your entries stay where they are.

Are the numbers certified?

No. CarbonLog gives estimates based on approximate emission factors. They are a good first step, but they are not a certified emissions report.

Who owns my data?

You do. You can edit or delete every entry, and Pro and Enterprise let you export everything as CSV.

What does “yearly” billing save?

Pro costs €120 a year instead of €144 when paid monthly, which is two months free.
