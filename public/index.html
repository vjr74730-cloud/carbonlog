Know your footprint.

CarbonLog turns your company’s energy, travel and purchases into CO₂ numbers you can act on, so people and nature can keep sharing the same valley.

Try a quick check

1Electricity & gas

Switch on the numbers

Type in the kWh or MWh from your energy bill. CarbonLog converts it into estimated CO₂ for you, so there is no maths to do.

≈ 0.38 kg CO₂ per kWh approximate factor

2Car, train & flights

Every trip counts

Log business trips in kilometres or miles. Pick how you travelled and the distance, and the footprint is calculated on the spot.

Train ≈ 0.035 · Flight ≈ 0.15 kg CO₂ per km approximate

3Things you buy

Spend that adds up

No supplier data needed. Enter what you spent in euros for a rough, spend-based estimate of what your purchases add.

≈ 0.40 kg CO₂ per € very rough

4Your yearly goal

Aim lower each year

Set a reduction target, for example 15 % less than last year, and watch your progress on the dashboard as you log.

Target: −15 % vs last year example

Good to know

Honest numbers for a first step

Estimates, not audits

CarbonLog gives you a close first estimate. It is not a certified emissions report.

Factors you can check

Every emission factor is listed in Settings with its source and year. Verify it against an official source before you report.

History stays put

Changing a factor never rewrites the entries you saved earlier.
